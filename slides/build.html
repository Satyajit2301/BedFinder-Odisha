🎯 Problem & Objective

During medical emergencies in Odisha, families drive blindly between hospitals facing "phantom beds"—arriving to find the last ICU or oxygen bed occupied minutes earlier.

Our Mission: Build a mathematically grounded, real-time triage system that couples urban transit time with stochastic bed turnover and financial scheme awareness (Ayushman Bharat / BSKY).

🔄 End-to-End Execution Pipeline
1

Multilingual Query Parsing (Rule-Based + Groq AI)

Extracts user landmark (Patia, Jaydev Vihar, Airport), scheme card status, and priority (cheapest, nearest, best rated) across English, Hindi, and Hinglish.

2

Dynamic Geospatial Routing

Haversine geodesic computation with urban traffic friction multipliers computes real-time driving distance and estimated time of arrival (ETA).

3

Poisson Queuing Arrival Probability Engine (P_bed)

Calculates the exact stochastic probability that a bed remains free upon arrival, taking into account current occupancy, average length of stay (ALOS), and transit time.

4

BedScore Multi-Attribute Ranking & Instant Render

Scores all 25 hospitals in <15ms; renders interactive OpenStreetMap pins, Top 3 hospital recommendations, and 4 Quick-Pick triage cards.
