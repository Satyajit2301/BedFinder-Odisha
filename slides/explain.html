📐 Formula 1: Mentor's Formula
Score = 1000·A + X/1000 − 100·Y − Z/10

Where:
• A = Free Bed Ratio (free / total % × 1000)
• X/1000 = (Rating × Reviews) ÷ 1000 (Normalized reputation)
• −100·Y = −100 × Road Distance in km (Urban travel penalty)
• −Z/10 = −Effective Admission Fee ÷ 10 in INR

✨ Formula 2: Probabilistic BedScore (Recommended)
BedScore = 100 × P_bed × (0.35·Trust + 0.40·Closeness + 0.25·Affordability)

Sub-factors strictly bounded in [0.0, 1.0]:
• Trust = 0.70·(rating/5) + 0.30·min(1, log₁₀(reviews+1)/3)
• Closeness = max(0, 1 - distance_km / 25)
• Affordability = Card: 1.0 | No Card: max(0, 1 - fee / 10000)

The Multiplicative Gatekeeper Guarantee:

Because P_bed multiplies the utility sum, if arrival bed probability falls to 0, BedScore drops to exactly 0.0—regardless of 5-star ratings or low prices. A hospital with no beds will never be recommended!

⏳ Queuing Physics: Transit-Decay Probability (P_bed)

Models hospital bed occupancy as an M/M/c continuous-time Markovian queue with Poisson turnover:

P_bed = Φ( (free_beds - 0.5) / σ )
σ = √( 2 × (occupied_beds / (ALOS × 24)) × ETA_hours )
• ALOS (Average Length of Stay): Set to 4.0 days based on Indian tertiary clinical data.
• Real-World Sensitivity: A hospital with 1 free bed 10 minutes away has P = 89%. The same hospital 60 minutes away drops to P = 69% due to turnover risk.
